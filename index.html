FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before working with us.

What services does Tinyverse Solutions offer?+
We offer AI automation, ERP system design, web development, graphics & brand design, social media design and management, and SEO & Google Ads. All services can be delivered individually or as an integrated package.
How long does a typical project take?+
Most projects go from kick-off to launch in 4–6 weeks. AI automation and ERP projects can take 6–10 weeks depending on complexity. We always give you a clear timeline in the proposal before work begins.
How much does it cost?+
Pricing depends on scope, but we work with businesses of all sizes. Web development starts from KES 80,000. AI automation projects start from KES 120,000. We always quote a fixed price upfront — no hourly billing surprises. Book a call for an accurate quote within 24 hours.
Do you work with small businesses or only large companies?+
Both. We work with early-stage startups and SMEs as well as established enterprises. Our pricing is structured to be accessible at each stage of growth.
What happens after the project launches?+
Every project includes a post-launch support period (typically 30 days). After that, we offer maintenance retainers for ongoing updates, monitoring and improvements. We don't build and disappear.
Can you integrate with M-Pesa and local payment systems?+
Yes — M-Pesa (STK Push, C2B, B2C), Pesapal, Flutterwave and other East African payment gateways are all within our stack. We handle the full integration including reconciliation and reporting.
What makes Tinyverse different from other agencies?+
Three things: We combine design, development AND AI automation under one roof. We're Kenya-based and deeply understand the East African market including M-Pesa integrations. And we guarantee fixed timelines and fixed prices — no surprises.
