What services does Tinyverse Solutions offer?+
AI automation, ERP systems, web development, brand design, social media management, and SEO. Each service works standalone or as part of an integrated package.
How long does a typical project take?+
Most projects launch in 4-6 weeks. AI and ERP work takes 6-10 weeks depending on complexity. You get a clear timeline in the proposal before we start.
How much does it cost?+
Web development starts from KES 49,999 (our Web + SEO package). AI automation from KES 120,000. Fixed price, always quoted upfront. Book a call and we'll scope it within 24 hours.
Do you work with small businesses or only large companies?+
Both. Early-stage startups to established enterprises. Pricing is structured to fit each stage of growth.
What happens after the project launches?+
Every project includes 30 days post-launch support. After that, maintenance retainers cover ongoing updates and monitoring. We don't build and disappear.
Can you integrate with M-Pesa and local payment systems?+
Yes. M-Pesa (STK Push, C2B, B2C), Stripe, Pesapal, and Flutterwave. Full integration including reconciliation and reporting.
What makes Tinyverse different?+
Design, development and AI automation under one roof. No hand-offs between teams, no communication delays. Fixed prices, fixed timelines. Most projects go live in 4-6 weeks, not months.
