Cookie Policy

Short version: we use minimal tracking and never sell your data.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit.

What we use

Our website uses one category of tracking only:

  • Analytics tracking: We record which pages you visit, your device type and your approximate timezone. This helps us understand how the site performs and where to improve it. No personally identifiable information is stored.

We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels or third-party tracking beyond our own analytics.

Session data

We store a temporary session ID in your browser's session storage to count unique visits accurately. This is deleted when you close your browser tab.

Controlling cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our website is fully functional without them: you simply will not be counted in our analytics.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here.
