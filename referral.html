No limit. KSh 5,000 per confirmed referral, every time. Refer 1 or refer 50 - the commission is the same.

Your link stays active for 30 days. If they click today and sign up within 30 days, you still get the commission.

Not at all. Share your link, we handle everything else. You just get paid when it closes.
